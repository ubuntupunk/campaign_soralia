# BackaBuddy campaign — compliance changes

Campaign: `stop-soralia-village-automatic-handovers`
Reviewed against BackaBuddy's T&Cs (prohibited-content list) on 2026-11-03.

## Why

The T&Cs prohibit, verbatim:

> "Details of a pending or current legal case."

The published story named **Case 518005**, its date, the forum, the order's
terms, the adjudicator's findings and the 15 July 2026 resolutions. That is a
summary of a live case, not a reference to one.

Secondary risk: **"Credit repair or debt settlement services."** The hardship
fund was described as "stabilising an account so collection fees stop
compounding", alongside funded "statement reviews" and "complaint support" —
which reads like a funded debt-settlement service.

Enforcement exposure: BackaBuddy reserves the right "to reject, cancel,
interrupt, remove, or suspend any campaign at any time and for any reason",
and to "permanently remove campaigns that present legal, ethical,
reputational, or safety risks". `isVerified` is false and `vettingStatus` is 0,
so the page is live but unreviewed.

## Do not link

`soraliavillagecampaign.org.za/csos-order` is titled "CSOS Adjudication Order —
Case 518005" and restates the order. The page itself is a truthful account of
a public record on our own site and needs no change. Do **not** link it from
the BackaBuddy campaign: reviewers follow links, and the link exports exactly
the content the story has just been cleaned of.

hard line on "debt settlement services", the fallback is to move both bullets
into the VSO's general "member support" line without naming statement reviews
or complaints. The substance survives; the words do not.
