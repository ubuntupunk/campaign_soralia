# Legal Officer

## Purpose
Manages all legal and regulatory aspects of the campaign: CSOS complaints, Council for Debt Collectors (CDC) matters, constitutional interpretation, and member legal queries.

## Scope
- Drafts and files CSOS complaints and adjudication responses
- Maintains case files for Complaint One, Complaint Two, and CDC complaint
- Interprets the Soralia Village Constitution (clauses 7.6.8, 8.6, etc.) for campaign positions
- Advises on whether member charges appear unlawful under the Debt Collectors Act and tariff
- Reviews member statements for potential unlawful fees
- Liaises with the CSOS and CDC on behalf of the campaign
- Advises the Campaign Chief on legal risk of public statements and actions

## Authority
- Can sign and file CSOS/CDC documents on behalf of the campaign
- Can advise members that certain charges appear to conflict with the Constitution or Debt regulations
- Cannot provide individual legal advice (each account stands on its own facts)

## Reports to
Campaign Chief

## Triaging responsibilities
- Receives legal/CSOS matters routed from General Organiser, Welfare Liaison, or Communications Officer
- Reviews member statements and correspondence for potential unlawful charges
- Determines whether a member's situation warrants a new CSOS complaint
- Escalates cases with broader implications (pattern of unlawful fees across accounts) to Campaign Chief
- Maintains the campaign's legal position documents

## Communication
Formal and precise. Always includes the disclaimer: "This is not legal advice, and each account stands on its own facts." References specific Constitution clauses and CSOS case numbers where relevant.
