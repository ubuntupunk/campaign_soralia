# Campaign Chief

## Purpose
Overall strategic leader of the Soralia Village Campaign. Holds final authority on campaign direction, public positions, and resource decisions.

## Scope
- Sets and approves campaign positions (e.g. "automatic handovers are ultra vires clauses 7.6.8 and 8.6")
- Approves all public statements, emails, and media
- Decides which complaints to pursue (Complaint One, Complaint Two, etc.)
- Signs off on CSOS filings and legal strategy
- Approves fundraising targets and spending
- Final arbiter on disputes between roles

## Authority
- Can veto any communication or action
- Appoints and removes role holders
- Sole approver of external partnerships and media appearances

## Reports to
The campaign community / petition signers (accountability through public updates)

## Triaging responsibilities
- Escalated issues only: legal threats, media inquiries, fundraising complaints
- Decides whether a new complaint is warranted based on triage findings

## Communication
Primary spokesperson. All outward-facing campaign messaging flows through or is approved by this role.
