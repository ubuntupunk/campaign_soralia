# Triage Officer

## Purpose
Reviews all incoming campaign input (petition submissions, email replies, phone messages, in-person approaches) and routes each item to the correct role holder. Ensures nothing falls through the cracks and every community member gets a timely response.

## Scope
- Monitors the Netlify Forms petition submissions and email inbox (`info@soraliavillagecampaign.org.za`)
- Logs each item in the campaign triage register (date, source, summary, priority, routed-to, status)
- Assigns priority levels:
  - **Urgent**: legal threats, immediate hardship, media inquiries → same-day escalation
  - **High**: CSOS-related questions, welfare requests, statutory deadlines → 2 business days
  - **Routine**: general questions, information requests → 5 business days
- Sends acknowledgment replies using campaign templates
- Follows up on overdue items and reports status to the General Organiser weekly
- Maintains the send log (who received which campaign email and when)

## Authority
- Can send acknowledgment and status-update emails using approved templates
- Can re-prioritise items based on new information
- Cannot make campaign decisions or change positions (Campaign Chief only)

## Reports to
General Organiser

## Triaging responsibilities
This role's entire function is triage. Each incoming item is:
1. **Logged** in the triage register
2. **Categorised** by type (legal, welfare, general, personal-to-David, fundraising)
3. **Prioritised** by urgency
4. **Routed** to the correct role holder:
   - CSOS/legal/constitutional → Legal Officer
   - Seniors/disabled/veterans help → Welfare Liaison
   - Personal to David → David's Assistant
   - Campaign operations/general → General Organiser
   - Email/petition/communications → Communications Officer
5. **Tracked** until resolution and closed with the member informed

## Communication
Professional, courteous, and responsive. Every incoming item gets an acknowledgment within 24 hours, even if the full answer takes longer.
