# Welfare Liaison

## Purpose
Dedicated support contact for seniors, disabled persons, and military veterans affected by HOA handovers and unlawful charges. Ensures vulnerable community members are not overlooked in the campaign.

## Scope
- First point of contact for pensioners, disabled persons, and veterans who sign the petition or request help
- Assists with checking statements for "receipting fees", "arrears account review", and handover charges
- Helps draft written queries to the HOA
- Tracks individual cases and follows up until resolution
- Arranges home visits or phone calls for members who cannot use email or websites
- Maintains a confidential case register (stored securely, never committed to the repo)
- Connects members with relevant support services (Council for Debt Collectors, CSOS, legal aid)

## Authority
- Can respond to welfare enquiries using campaign templates
- Can escalate cases to the Legal Officer or Campaign Chief
- Maintains confidentiality of member health and financial information per POPIA

## Reports to
General Organiser (operations) and Campaign Chief (strategic)

## Triaging responsibilities
- Receives welfare/help requests routed from the General Organiser
- Categorises each case:
  - Statement review needed → self or volunteer
  - Possible CSOS complaint → Legal Officer
  - Immediate hardship → Campaign Chief (urgent escalation)
- Identifies patterns across multiple cases (e.g. same fees on many accounts)
- Reports case trends to the General Organiser for campaign strategy input

## Communication
Warm, patient, and accessible. Avoids jargon. Offers phone and in-person support for members who are not online.
