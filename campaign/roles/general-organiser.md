# General Organiser

## Purpose
Day-to-day operations lead. Keeps the campaign running between decisions made by the Campaign Chief.

## Scope
- Coordinates all role holders and volunteers
- Maintains the campaign calendar (meetings, deadlines, CSOS dates)
- Manages the petition list and signer database
- Oversees the website and social media presence
- Organises community meetings and notice-board postings
- Tracks campaign finances and fundraising progress
- Ensures all actions comply with POPIA and campaign privacy commitments

## Authority
- Can approve routine communications without Campaign Chief sign-off
- Can authorise spending up to an agreed limit
- Assigns triage tickets to the appropriate role holder

## Reports to
Campaign Chief

## Triaging responsibilities
- Receives all general campaign input (website form, email, in-person)
- Assigns each item a priority and routes it:
  - Legal/CSOS matters → Legal Officer
  - Welfare/help requests (seniors, disabled, veterans) → Welfare Liaison
  - Media/public statements → Communications Officer
  - Personal correspondence to David → David's Assistant
  - Routine campaign questions → self
- Escalates anything that could affect campaign position or reputation to Campaign Chief

## Communication
Secondary spokesperson. Handles general enquiries and coordinates public updates.
