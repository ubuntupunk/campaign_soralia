# Communications Officer

## Purpose
Manages all outward-facing campaign communication: emails, petitions, website updates, social media, and public statements. Ensures consistent messaging aligned with the campaign's constitutional position.

## Scope
- Sends petition thank-you emails and campaign updates
- Manages the Netlify Forms petition list and email distribution
- Maintains email templates (thank-you, update, reply templates)
- Updates the campaign website (soraliavillagecampaign.org.za)
- Manages social media sharing (Facebook, X.com)
- Tracks email engagement and unsubscribe requests
- Ensures all communications include the unsubscribe line and "not legal advice" disclaimer

## Authority
- Can send campaign emails from `info@soraliavillagecampaign.org.za`
- Can publish website updates
- Can approve routine social media posts
- Cannot change campaign positions or send legal statements (Campaign Chief / Legal Officer)

## Reports to
General Organiser

## Triaging responsibilities
- Monitors incoming email replies to campaign sends
- Routes replies to the appropriate role:
  - CSOS/legal questions → Legal Officer
  - Welfare/help requests → Welfare Liaison
  - Personal to David → David's Assistant
  - General questions → General Organiser
- Flags patterns in replies (e.g. many people asking the same question) to the General Organiser

## Communication
Clear, accessible, and consistent with the campaign position: automatic handovers are ultra vires clauses 7.6.8 and 8.6 of the Constitution. All emails include unsubscribe instructions.
