Subject: Soralia Village Community Action — Statement on CSOS 518015: the interim committee and the fresh election

Dear member,

Soralia Village Community Action has published a statement on the CSOS adjudication order in Case 518015, delivered on 13 November 2026. It deals with the interim committee, the fresh general meeting and the election of office-bearers.

Read the full statement here: https://soraliavillagecampaign.org.za/csos-518015-statement.html

In brief:

**The interim committee has no mandate from the past AGM.** The order declares that the resolutions passed by electronic ballot at the 13 July 2026 AGM are procedurally defective and of no force pending confirmation (paragraph 7.1). The mandate drawn from that meeting has expired. The trustees remain in office only as a caretaker arrangement "so that the HOA is not left without a committee" (paragraph 6.14). In substance the effect is the same as a dissolution of that mandate: a fresh election must be held, and the nomination of trustees must be considered at it (paragraph 7.5).

**A fresh meeting must be held within 30 days.** It must be on at least 14 days' written notice, conducted by show of hands under clause 5.6, and it must reconsider all the resolutions purportedly passed on 13 July, including the number of trustees and the election of the committee (paragraph 7.2). Every nomination must be considered by the presiding officer without discrimination and only under the criteria in clauses 6.1 and 6.2 — owner or spouse. There is no "paid-up" requirement.

**Why show of hands.** Clause 5.6 prescribes it. A visible vote lets everyone present see the level of support for a resolution or a candidate. We reject secret ballots and email or online preference polls; none is authorised by the Constitution, and changing the method requires a clause 12 amendment and the City's approval under clause 16.3.

**The ballot failure is on the HOA's own record.** The HOA told CSOS that paper ballots issued to members without digital access were "processed and captured into the voting system after the meeting" (paragraph 5.4.2). The adjudicator found that the results declared at the meeting were declared before the votes of all those present had been counted (paragraph 6.9), and recorded again that the results were announced before the paper ballots were captured (paragraph 6.29).

**Our Constitution is the basis on which we organise.** Soralia is a common-law homeowners' association, not a body corporate under the Body Corporates Act. We recognise the need to refresh the Constitution, but that must be done openly by the procedure it prescribes. We reject the latest attempt to circumvent that process and the process ordered by the CSOS.

**Our standing position.** We again urge members and supporters to reject automatic handovers and breaches of our constitutional dispensation and democratic character. We support legitimate debt collection aimed at restoring the village's finances, and we urge members to pay their levies on time. What we oppose is the unlawful circumvention of due process and action contrary to the Constitution and the Debt Collectors Act — not legitimate collection.

We are also tabling a member motion on further irregularities, including presiding authority at general meetings under clause 5.5. A copy is at https://soraliavillagecampaign.org.za/draft-resolution-clause-5-5.docx. Please ask the trustees to put it on the agenda of the fresh meeting.

This statement is not legal advice. The CSOS order itself is the authoritative text, and each member's position stands on its own facts.
