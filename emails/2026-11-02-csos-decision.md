## Plain-text version

Dear neighbour,

Thank you for signing the Soralia Village petition.

The first of the two CSOS complaints has now been decided. On 2 November 2026, the Community Schemes Ombud Service (CSOS) issued an adjudication order in Case 518005.

**What the order says**

The HOA was directed to reverse the "receipting fees" charged to the account that was the subject of the complaint for May, June, July and August 2026.

It was also directed to reverse the "arrears account review" charges for June, July and August 2026, and to recalculate the interest charged on those amounts.

The HOA must provide an updated statement reflecting the reversals and interest adjustments within 21 days of the order.

The adjudicator found that the charges had not been substantiated under the applicable debt collectors' tariff, and that resolutions adopted on 15 July 2026 could not retrospectively authorise earlier charges.

**What the order does not decide**

The adjudicator declined to rule on Trafalgar's practices across other schemes, because that falls outside what CSOS can grant. The order does not decide whether automatic handovers are lawful in general.

This order relates only to the account that was the subject of Complaint One. It does not automatically reverse charges on every account. Please check your own statement and query anything unclear in writing.

**Our position remains**

Automatic handovers are ultra vires clauses 7.6.8 and 8.6 of our Constitution.

Clause 8.6 says the committee "may" hand over a debt outstanding for more than 30 days. Clause 7.6.8 requires committee approval for routine recovery action. Each handover should be a committee decision on an individual account. The 15 July 2026 Hand Over Resolution cannot override those requirements.
