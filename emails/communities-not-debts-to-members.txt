Subject: Soralia Village Community Action — Communities, Not Debt Collection Schemes

Dear member,

We have published a new article, "Communities, Not Debt Collection Schemes". It sets out why Soralia Village is a community of people and households governed by its Constitution — not a debt-collection scheme with gardens and security gates attached.

Read it here:
https://soraliavillagecampaign.org.za/communities-not-debts.html

In brief:

- The Constitution binds the association, its committee and anyone acting for it, including a managing agent who chairs a meeting. In Case 518015 the adjudicator declared the 13 July 2026 electronic vote invalid pending confirmation and ordered a fresh general meeting by show of hands.

- The financial record is serious without being misquoted. The 2025 audit was unmodified, with an Emphasis of Matter and an inability to establish the recoverability of R454,557 in levies in arrears. Utilities cost R1,999,688 in 2025, and the 2026 budget assumes only a 45% recovery rate on water and sewerage. That shortfall is carried by the scheme, and in the end by every household.

- Clauses 7.6.8 and 8.6 vest a handover decision in the committee. They do not authorise an automatic calendar trigger. Our position is that receipting fees are irrational, opportunistic and not supported by the Debt Collectors Act: they penalise a member for paying. The CSOS order reversed the specified fees in the account before it and did not decide the wider question; we say this as our position, not as a finding attributed to the order.

- We will press the City of Cape Town on individual metering, municipal arrears, bulk-meter reconciliation and hardship relief, while putting the arrears book back under a written, minuted committee process. Hardship should be assessed case by case, with priority for seniors, people with disabilities and veterans.

- The clause 5.5 motion reaffirming that only the elected Chairperson — or a homeowner elected by the meeting — may preside has been proposed and seconded. It is ready for the agenda of the fresh meeting; it is not an adopted resolution. A copy is at https://soraliavillagecampaign.org.za/draft-resolution-clause-5-5.docx

Our standing position is unchanged: we support legitimate debt collection aimed at restoring the village's finances, and we urge members to pay their levies on time. What we oppose is the unlawful circumvention of due process and action contrary to the Constitution and the Debt Collectors Act — not legitimate collection.

This email and the article are not legal advice. The certified Constitution and the law prevail where anything conflicts, and each member's position stands on its own facts.
