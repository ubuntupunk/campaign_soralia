Why I complained to CSOS

Our position is clear: automatic handovers are ultra vires clauses 7.6.8 and 8.6 of the Soralia Village Constitution. Clause 8.6 permits the committee to hand over a debt only if it has been outstanding for more than 30 days, and only by an active committee decision. Clause 7.6.8 requires committee approval for each routine recovery action. A blanket resolution or calendar trigger cannot replace those individual decisions.

Lets get one thing straight. I don't owe anyone a cent
on my property. My home is 100% paid up. My current levy account is also paid
up.

My record of paying is sound and until the move from Residentia, there were
no 'strikes' against me as such.

Enter the City Debt Collectors and their aggressive 'Strike' action.

I was thus amazed to be treated like a bonded servant by Trafalgar Property, (instead of a community
member). An HOA is not a pure debt collecting machine and is rather a community of home-owners.
The period of dispossession in which certain individuals lacked common law rights is long gone,
in its place has arisen a culture of contempt for our Republic and its laws.

After querying the imposition of 'receipting fees' on my account with no basis in law, I received
responses that were incoherent and illogical to say the least. Accordingly, I
was informed that our HOA had appointed Trafalgar as debt collectors, and they
were acting in that capacity. In addition I was told, there was now an 'automatic
handover after 7 days' in place (our constitution stipulates at least 30 days)

That was not a mere procedural slip. An automatic handover by calendar date is
ultra vires clause 8.6, which permits the committee to hand over a debt only once
it has been outstanding for more than 30 days, and only by an active committee
decision. It also bypasses clause 7.6.8, which requires committee approval for
each routine recovery action.

I proceeded to contest the receipting fees, requesting sight of the 'Handover
resolution' and thus the underlying jurist basis for the fees.
My position is that such fees would only be possible if my account was formerly handed over,
and even then, such a category as 'receipting fees' does not exist in the current Debt regulations.

What transpired could be considered a circus comedy, if the implications were
not so dire. Trafalgar forwarded me documents from a totally different scheme
called Soralis Place, with a completely different board.

When I pointed this out, they doubled-down, claiming, 'this is the correct scheme' and 'trustees
change from time to time'.

On July 15 I received a letter of demand for the
outstanding fees, and I proceeded to file two complaints before CSOS, the first
deals with the receipting fees and failure to abide by the regulations on debt
collection, the second regarding the dissolution of the water committee without
notice and the imposition of a novel voting system in breach of the
constitution, which resulted in paper ballots being discarded, with digital voting rendering instantaneous but spurious outcomes.

I have filed an additional complaint before the Council of Debt
Collectors (CDC) regarding the improper and unlawful actions of Trafalgar. Their latest
response to my first CSOS complaint, is incoherent to say the least. It attaches
a new resolution by the Board governing handovers seeking to implement the
resolution retroactively whilst making out a case that I am somehow a
recalcitrant debtor.

That 15 July 2026 Hand Over Resolution is itself ultra vires clause 7.6.8. It
removes the per-case committee approval our Constitution requires and delegates
complete discretion to Trafalgar. A trustee resolution cannot override an express
provision of the Constitution.

I am not here to oppose lawful fees, but rather the strange
notion that anybody should deem fit to aggressively pursue my household on
account of membership of an HOA, via a strategy which is essentially an abuse of the system.

I therefore urge you all to read the associated
campaign article, (my CSOS docs will be available in due course) and to consider the ramifications of the actions of current board and the
managing agents.

Sincerely yours,
