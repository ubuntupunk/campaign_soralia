About the Campaign

The Soralia Village Campaign stands united against automatic handovers of arrears accounts that threaten our most vulnerable community members. We believe in fair treatment and social assessment before any action is taken against pensioners, disabled persons, and military veterans.

Our community deserves dignity, not displacement. We demand that the Board conducts proper social assessments before making decisions that affect people's lives and livelihoods.

Know Your Rights

Right to Fair Process

No one should face automatic handovers without proper assessment of their circumstances.

Right to Social Assessment

The Board must evaluate individual situations before taking action on arrears accounts.

Right to Dignity

Vulnerable members deserve respect and protection, not punitive measures.
