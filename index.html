About the Campaign

The Soralia Village Campaign stands united against automatic handovers of arrears accounts that threaten our most vulnerable community members. Our position is that automatic handovers are ultra vires clauses 7.6.8 and 8.6 of the Soralia Village Constitution — handovers require an active committee decision for each account (clause 8.6) and committee approval for routine recovery action (clause 7.6.8). Blanket or automatic handovers are not authorised.

We believe in fair treatment and social assessment before any action is taken against pensioners, disabled persons, and military veterans. Our community deserves dignity, not displacement.

Our Position: Ultra Vires the Constitution

Automatic handovers are not merely harsh. They are ultra vires clauses 7.6.8 and 8.6 of the Soralia Village Constitution. The 15 July 2026 Hand Over Resolution cannot override the Constitution.

Clause 8.6

The committee “may” hand over a debt only if it has been outstanding for more than 30 days. That is a discretionary committee decision on a specific account — not a calendar trigger, automatic rule, or blanket handover.

Clause 7.6.8

Routine legal recovery requires committee approval for each action. The 15 July 2026 Hand Over Resolution removes that per-case approval and delegates complete discretion to a third party. It is therefore ultra vires.

A resolution or a managing agent cannot replace the individual committee decision our Constitution requires. Every handover must be decided account by account.

Know Your Rights

Right to Constitutional Process

Automatic handovers bypass the committee decisions required by clauses 7.6.8 and 8.6. They are ultra vires our Constitution.

Right to Social Assessment

The Board must evaluate individual situations before taking action on arrears accounts.

Right to Dignity

Vulnerable members deserve respect and protection, not punitive measures.
